## Creative brief

Create a near-frontal view across Pelagos Relay, a fictional coastal communications depot built from repurposed concrete warehouses. The environment combines sun-bleached beige stucco, poured concrete, corrugated shutters, unbranded wooden crates, chain-link fencing, overhead cables, canvas covers, dusty paving, pale sky, and sea haze. Warm late-afternoon light dominates, with cool architectural shadows and restrained rust-orange details.

The reel console is supplied by the application and must not appear in the artwork. Keep the middle third behind the reels low-detail and low-contrast. Put shutters, crates, fencing, and architectural silhouettes at the outer edges. The scene should evoke a grounded competitive tactical-game environment without copying a map, composition, prop set, battlefield, military base, casino floor, or identifiable real location.
