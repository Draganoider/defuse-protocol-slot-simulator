## Creative brief

Create a rugged fictional equipment-recovery case from Pelagos Relay. It has a compact rectangular profile, faded sand-colored polymer shell, graphite rubber corners, ordinary steel latches, worn canvas handle, one diagonal muted orange strap, and a blank inventory plate. It must look like practical field instrumentation rather than a weapon case, ammunition container, cash case, loot box, or recognizable commercial product.

Make the handle, latches, strap, and corner guards form a bold silhouette that survives small-scale rendering. Surface wear belongs at seams, feet, and handles while the face remains simple and legible. Do not include text, currency, prize imagery, or symbols suggesting real-world ownership or affiliation.

## Composition and delivery

- Centered three-quarter product shot with the handle, latches, and orange strap fully visible.
- Square-safe subject with at least 8% clear margin on every edge.
- Transparent background with a clean silhouette and no baked platform or environment.
- Neutral studio light with a restrained warm late-afternoon edge.
- One primary case silhouette, a sand/graphite material family, and one muted orange accent.
- Runtime delivery: 512×512 WebP with alpha; no raw generation dump is retained in the public repository.

## Accessibility and animation handoff

- Recognizable at 128 px and tolerable at 96 px in color and grayscale.
- Latch geometry, strap, and handle position must carry identity without accent colors.
- Win highlights and reward-gold tier effects remain separate from the base texture.
- Avoid tiny latches, labels, and reflections that disappear during reel motion.
